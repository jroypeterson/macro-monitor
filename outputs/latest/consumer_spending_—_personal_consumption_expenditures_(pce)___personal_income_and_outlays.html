Definitions
  • Real Personal Consumption Expenditures (PCEC96): inflation- adjusted consumer spending -- the volume of what households actually bought, the biggest block of GDP.
  • Personal Consumption Expenditures (PCE): consumer spending in current dollars -- nominal outlays, before stripping out inflation.
  • PCE Price Index (PCEPI): the Fed's preferred headline inflation gauge -- broader than CPI and re-weights as spending patterns shift.
  • Core PCE Price Index (PCEPILFE): PCE inflation excluding food and energy -- the specific number the Fed's 2% inflation target refers to.
