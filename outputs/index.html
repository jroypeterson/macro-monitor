📊 macro-monitor

Disciplined US macro feed for the #macro Slack channel. Posts release headlines + charts for 9 Tier A families (CPI, payrolls, PCE, claims, GDP, JOLTS, PPI, retail sales, ECI).

📈 Current state dashboard
Latest value per Tier A family with mini-chart and links to each release report. Refreshed after every release post.
📅 2026 annual macro calendar
Year-long Tier A release schedule, month-by-month grid. Refreshed quarterly.
🌍 Global macro dashboard
Latest CPI / GDP / unemployment / policy rates for the eurozone, UK, China & Japan. Refreshed weekly.
🏥 Healthcare & biopharma (hc_macro_policy)
Full HC-environment cockpit on the hc-macro-policy-pages site: HC operating dashboard · CMS rule-cycle calendar · CMS rates (incl. MA) · NIH funding · Managed-care MLR · Coverage / enrollment · NFP hospital medians · Policy timeline · State Medicaid · China biopharma · Biopharma R&D · Biotech VC · Trade-group surveys.
📂 Local galleries (readable/)
Open locally (not deployed to Pages): Ahead-of-the-Curve, market valuation/factor charts, the government & healthcare spending and drug-price inflation charts. See readable/index.html for the full local hub.
