Definitions
  • Nonfarm Payrolls (PAYEMS): total US jobs added or lost in the month across all employers except farms -- the headline jobs number from the BLS Employment Situation.
  • U-3 Unemployment Rate (UNRATE): the official/headline unemployment rate — people with no job who actively looked for work in the prior 4 weeks, as a % of the civilian labor force.
  • U-6 Underemployment Rate (U6RATE): the broadest BLS labor-underutilization measure — the U-3 unemployed PLUS marginally attached workers (want a job, looked in the past year but not the past 4 weeks, incl. discouraged workers) PLUS people working part-time for economic reasons (want full-time work but can't get the hours), as a % of the labor force plus the marginally attached.
  • Health Care Employment (HC_TOTAL, computed): sum of the Ambulatory Care + Hospitals + Nursing & Residential Care payroll series (NAICS 621+622+623) -- total healthcare jobs, a direct staffing/capacity read for provider names.
  • White-Collar Employment (WC_TOTAL, computed): sum of the Professional & Business Services (USPBS) + Information (USINFO) + Financial Activities (USFIRE) payroll supersectors — a proxy for office/knowledge-worker jobs, shown as YoY % change plus whether that growth rate is accelerating or decelerating vs the prior month and vs 3 months ago.
